🛡️ Privacy & Voice Architecture

1. Speech Recognition (STT):
Audio is transcribed 100% locally on your machine using Faster-Whisper (CTranslate2). No microphone audio is ever transmitted to third-party clouds or external servers.


2. Audio Storage Policy:
Zero Audio Retention. Ephemeral audio buffers are decoded in-memory and immediately destroyed after transcription. No microphone recordings are ever saved to disk.


3. Text-to-Speech (TTS):
Sensei's answers are converted to natural speech via Microsoft Edge Neural TTS over secure HTTPS. Only the tutor's response text is sent for audio synthesis (₹0 budget, no user tracking).


4. Transcription Review:
You can review, edit, or cancel every voice transcription before it is submitted to the language model.
